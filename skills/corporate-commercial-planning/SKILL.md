# Corporate commercial planning

Use the organization and product facts provided. Separate confirmed problems, assumptions and questions. Draft material for internal review, using placeholders for missing names or contact details. Return a file or a decision table to the requester.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include account-research, call-prep, contact-research, create-an-asset.

## Try it

A sample customer has 20 licensed seats, 12 active and a renewal in six weeks.

**Expected result.** A review brief shows 60 percent active, names missing adoption context and identifies one internal follow-up.

**Limit.** Sending messages, enriching individuals and accessing accounts require a different scope and are outside these local workflows.
