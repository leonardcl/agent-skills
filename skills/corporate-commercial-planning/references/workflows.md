# Corporate commercial planning workflows

## account-research

Summarize a fictional or public organization’s product, buying constraints and observable need from supplied sources. Distinguish a known requirement from a sales hypothesis and list one corroborating source.

## call-prep

Turn the account brief into a local meeting plan with objective, unresolved assumptions and neutral discovery questions. Associate each question with the decision its answer affects.

## contact-research

Use supplied fictional roles to map who requests, evaluates and approves a purchase. Flag unknown responsibility as a question. Keep the result at role level without collecting identities.

## create-an-asset

Select the buyer question a one-page collateral draft should answer. Support the response with supplied evidence, include limitations and verify that every claim matches the approved product brief.

## daily-briefing

Sort a fictional opportunity snapshot by deadline, changed risk and pending decision. Highlight changes since the prior snapshot and distinguish a missing update from confirmed inactivity.

## escalation

Classify a fictional issue by user impact, scope and urgency. Attach the shortest reproduction and evidence. Name the owner, immediate workaround and next promised status checkpoint.

## knowledge-management

Turn a verified support resolution into a searchable article with symptoms, applicability, steps and success condition. Check a similar but different case to define the boundary.

## onboarding-playbook

Define the customer’s first independent success and the prerequisites for it. Include both staff and customer owners, a way to verify completion and a recovery route when a step stalls.

## qbr-builder

Reconcile the quarter’s stated goals against outcomes using matched windows. Separate adoption signals from proven value and draft a review agenda with one concrete next-quarter decision.

## response-drafting

Draft a response from the supplied ticket and verified product behavior. State what happened, what the user can do and what remains open. Keep the draft local for review.

## sequence-load

Validate a fictional sequence plan offline. Check stage order, eligibility, duplicate enrollment and exit conditions. Return a dry-run table of who would qualify without importing or sending anything.

## slack-messaging

Turn supplied facts into a local channel-message draft for the intended audience. Identify the decision up front and one responsible role. Keep mentions and delivery out of the exercise.

## slack-search

Search only a selected redacted message export. Cite message IDs and dates, distinguish a proposal from approval and report when the supplied window contains no answer.

## ticket-triage

Classify a supplied support ticket by affected task, severity, reproducibility and owner. Merge duplicates by symptom and evidence while preserving distinct affected cases.

## weekly-prep-brief

Group supplied opportunities by next decision, owner and evidence age. Reconcile the weekly totals to the underlying records and mark which blockers require a decision this week.
