# Corporate decisions

State who must decide what and by when. Compare the available options, including deferral, against the binding constraint. In the decision record give a recommendation, evidence owner, unresolved dependency and trigger for reconsideration. For a quoted market valuation use Public market research.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include board-meeting-prep, business-case-builder, change-management, competitive-analysis.

See the [worked committee packet](references/decision-packet.md) for the arithmetic and decision boundary.

## Try it

A committee has 12 staff-days. Plan A uses 10 and needs an unsigned venue. B uses 8 and has a confirmed room.

**Expected result.** The packet separates the feasible fallback from the conditional choice and assigns a deadline for confirming the venue.

**Limit.** Board authorization and approval of a proposed decision remain with the responsible people.
