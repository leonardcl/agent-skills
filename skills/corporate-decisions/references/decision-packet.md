# A decision packet with a real fallback

An example committee can allocate 12 staff-days. Option A needs 10 days and access to an unconfirmed venue. Option B needs 8 days and the room is booked.

| Decision input | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remaining team capacity after allocation | 2 staff-days | 4 staff-days |
| Venue evidence | Request sent, confirmation missing | Booking received |
| Reversal condition | Written confirmation before the venue deadline | Revisit if confirmed A offers a benefit sufficient to justify 2 more days |

Ask the committee to choose whether it values A's stated benefit enough to hold a conditional reservation. If the owner cannot articulate a benefit or the venue deadline passes, B is the feasible choice in the supplied packet. A vote authorizing A and a vote granting the reservation are different decisions. Attach the source confirming each prerequisite.
