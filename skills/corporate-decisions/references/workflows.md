# Corporate decisions workflows

## board-meeting-prep

Split agenda items into decisions and information. Give each decision an owner, alternatives, requested vote and reading deadline. Schedule the decision before its downstream dependency and verify that each requested vote has supporting material.

## business-case-builder

Compare the current process with a proposed investment over the same horizon. Separate one-time costs, recurring costs and benefits. Identify the adoption level at which incremental value turns positive.

## change-management

Map the affected roles from their current procedure to the proposed one. Pilot with a cohort whose baseline is known. Count unresolved exceptions and name the condition for pausing rollout.

## competitive-analysis

Compare two alternatives on the same buyer task. Cite observed capabilities, contract terms and unknowns separately. Show the criterion whose change would reverse the recommendation.

## deep-research

Break a decision into answerable questions and assign each supplied public document to a question. Trace assertions to passages, flag contradictions and identify the specific missing source that could settle them.

## executive-communication

Lead an internal draft with the decision, consequence and action owner. Check that each numeric claim traces to the same reporting period and that pending decisions are clearly labeled.

## internal-comms

For each affected audience record what changes, when and who answers exceptions. Draft a short internal notice from that table and verify that someone in each audience can identify their next step.

## knowledge-synthesis

Create a claim-to-source matrix. Merge repeated reports only when their underlying evidence is independent or identified as shared. Preserve conflicting dates and show which decision depends on resolving them.

## kpi-dashboard

For each decision, define the metric numerator, denominator, time window, owner and intervention threshold. Recalculate a sample cell from raw counts, including an empty-denominator case.

## ma-due-diligence

List the acquisition thesis and assumptions that make the combination worthwhile. Tie each material assumption to a document, reviewer and disconfirming observation. Describe integration dependencies and unresolved liabilities.

## risk-assessment

Describe each business event, driver, plausible consequence and current control. Rank using one agreed scale. Separate gross impact from remaining exposure and record a response owner.

## strategic-planning

Set a time horizon and one measurable outcome. Compare paths against capacity and dependencies. Give each selected initiative a baseline, target, owner and stop condition.

## task-management

Turn decisions into tasks with a verb, owner, due condition and evidence of completion. Keep a dependency explicit when a task cannot start. Reconcile open work against the original commitments.
