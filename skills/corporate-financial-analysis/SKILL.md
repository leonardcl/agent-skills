# Corporate financial analysis

Identify the decision and the reporting period. Preserve source units, sign conventions and currencies. Build an assumptions table so a reviewer can change a driver and trace the result into the output. Keep projected amounts distinct from recognized results.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include 3-statements, buyer-list, check-model, cim-builder.

## Try it

A product sells for 12 units, costs 5 per unit to deliver and requires 140 of fixed costs.

**Expected result.** Show 7 contribution per unit and break-even at 20 units. State which costs the calculation excludes.

**Limit.** A worked model is a planning artifact. Tax, valuation and transaction conclusions depend on qualified review and current facts.
