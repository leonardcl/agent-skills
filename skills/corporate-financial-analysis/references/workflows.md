# Corporate financial analysis workflows

## 3-statements

Link net income to retained earnings and operating cash flow. Reconcile ending cash with opening cash plus all cash flows. Check that assets equal liabilities plus equity in every modeled period.

## buyer-list

Use supplied fictional organization profiles. Rank potential buyers by strategic fit, capacity and conflicts. Give a source or unknown for each criterion and keep personal contact details outside the analysis.

## check-model

Trace a headline output backward to inputs, confirm units and verify subtotal signs. Change one driver in a copy, then test that related statements move and unrelated measures remain stable.

## cim-builder

Build a company overview, operating history, drivers, risks and assumptions from the supplied packet. Reconcile summary numbers to the model and flag any unverified statement before circulating a draft.

## client-report

Write a fictional period report from supplied opening, closing and contribution data. Reconcile cash flows before attributing performance. State the benchmark and measurement window on the same page.

## client-review

Prepare an internal review agenda with stated objectives, prior commitments, changes in circumstances and unanswered questions. Distinguish the observed figures from any further decisions requiring professional review.

## comps-analysis

Choose peers under explicit operating and accounting criteria. Align enterprise or equity value with its corresponding denominator, date and currency. Show the median and the effect of removing an outlier.

## datapack-builder

Inventory supplied files by period, source, sensitivity and model use. Reconcile totals across tabs. Return an index with missing periods and version conflicts before drawing conclusions.

## dcf-model

Project explicit free cash flows under documented drivers, discount each at a rate consistent with its currency and claim, then add discounted terminal value. Show sensitivity to growth and discount rate and reconcile the enterprise-to-equity bridge.

## dd-checklist

For each thesis assumption identify the confirming record, reviewer and due date. Mark received and verified separately. Prioritize unresolved items that could alter value or feasibility.

## dd-meeting-prep

Convert unresolved diligence items into questions tied to a specific source discrepancy. Assign a question owner and the document or answer needed to close it. Keep the meeting draft local.

## deal-screening

Apply the stated mandate and hard exclusions before scoring attractiveness. Record a pass, fail or missing-input result for each requirement. Show which missing item could change the screening decision.

## deal-sourcing

Filter a supplied fictional company universe against the mandate and rationale. Keep reasons for both inclusions and exclusions. Produce a research shortlist with no enrichment or contact action.

## deal-tracker

Use a stable deal ID, stage definition, owner, last evidence and next decision. Reconcile duplicates before reporting counts. Separate formally closed deals from those merely inactive.

## financial-plan

Model an illustrative cash budget from opening balance, income, fixed obligations and variable outflows. Locate the first period below the chosen cash floor. Compare a delayed receipt with the base case.

## ic-memo

Write an internal committee memo with thesis, evidence, alternatives, downside and unresolved diligence. Link valuation assumptions to the model and name a fact that would invalidate the thesis.

## investment-proposal

Compare fictional alternatives against an explicit objective, horizon, downside tolerance and liquidity need. Show assumptions and sensitivity. Deliver an illustrative decision document without personalized advice or execution.

## invoice-organizer

Index supplied sample invoices by issuer, invoice number, currency, date and amount. Detect duplicates using the compound identity and reconcile totals by currency without overwriting the originals.

## lbo-model

Reconcile purchase uses to financing sources. Schedule interest and debt reduction using cash available after operations. Compare entry equity with exit equity under a downside case and flag impossible repayments.

## merger-model

Build separate standalone cases first. Add financing, incremental shares, integration cost and explicitly timed synergies. Compare combined earnings per share with acquirer standalone and reconcile the change.

## model-update

Freeze the prior forecast, replace only newly reported periods with sourced actuals and reconcile the variance. Change future assumptions separately so the reader can distinguish actualization from revised judgment.

## pitch-deck

Turn the internal thesis into a sequence of decision, evidence, economics, downside and next diligence step. Reconcile each chart with the same model version and remove claims unsupported by the packet.

## portfolio-monitoring

Calculate illustrative exposures from a supplied holdings snapshot and timestamped prices. Compare concentration and drift with stated example limits. Report stale or missing valuations explicitly.

## portfolio-rebalance

Compare hypothetical current and target weights on the same valuation date. Calculate percentage-point drift and reconcile both columns to 100 percent. Return a scenario table without instruments for placing orders.

## process-letter

Convert the fictional process timetable into deadlines, required materials, evaluation criteria and clarification channels. Check every required submission against a deadline and draft the letter for internal review.

## returns-analysis

Separate starting value, contributions, distributions and ending value. Select a return measure suited to the timing of flows. Mark periods lacking sufficient valuation data and reconcile the cash-flow signs.

## strip-profile

Prepare comparable one-row profiles using the same source date, company scope, operating units and financial definitions. Leave an explicit missing value wherever the packet lacks a number.

## tax-loss-harvesting

In a fictional lot ledger, separate cost basis, current value and unrealized difference per lot. Reconcile share counts and flag jurisdiction, holding-period and replacement assumptions for review. Produce arithmetic only.

## teaser

Draft a fictional anonymized business summary using only approved figures. Check that distinctive customer names, locations and unapproved forecasts do not re-identify the subject.

## unit-economics

Define the unit, period and cost boundary. Subtract attributable variable cost from realized unit revenue and compare contribution with acquisition cost. Sensitize one driver and identify the break-even point.

## value-creation-plan

Translate each initiative into a baseline, operational lever, expected financial line, timing and owner. Eliminate double counting where two initiatives affect the same margin improvement.
