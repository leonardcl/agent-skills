# Corporate legal procurement

Separate mandatory terms from scored preferences. Cite the clause, requirement or supplier response supporting each row. Record unknowns and route material legal interpretations to the decision owner or counsel.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include canned-responses, compliance, compliance-tracking, contract-negotiation.

## Try it

Supplier A costs 15 with delivery in ten days. Supplier B costs 17 with delivery in four. The event needs delivery in five days.

**Expected result.** The decision table rules out A for the specified deadline and records which B terms still need confirmation.

**Limit.** A checklist organizes evidence. Local examples do not establish jurisdiction-specific legal obligations.
