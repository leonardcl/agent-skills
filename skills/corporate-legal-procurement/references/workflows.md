# Corporate legal procurement workflows

## canned-responses

Classify a fictional inquiry by issue and sensitivity. Draft an acknowledgement using approved facts, with a routing condition when the question exceeds the template. Keep legal conclusions open for counsel.

## compliance

Map provided requirements to the affected process, evidence owner and record. Distinguish control design from observed operation. Flag missing proof and ask the owner to validate the interpretation.

## compliance-tracking

Record each supplied obligation with effective date, renewal condition and responsible role. Reconcile completed tasks to dated evidence and identify what expires before the next review.

## contract-negotiation

Create a term sheet with must-haves, negotiable points and fallback positions. Model the cost of each concession and distinguish draft positions from authorized commitments.

## contract-review

Compare supplied clauses against an agreed baseline. Quote the relevant clause number, describe the practical consequence and propose a question or revision for counsel. Distinguish missing terms from explicit exceptions.

## inventory-forecasting

Project units on hand from beginning inventory, known receipts and demand by period. Locate the first shortage against lead time and test a delayed shipment scenario.

## legal-risk-assessment

Describe a fact pattern and the possible business consequence under the supplied rules. Separate severity from likelihood, note the jurisdiction and flag unresolved interpretation for counsel.

## meeting-briefing

Summarize the decision needed, parties by role, disputed clauses and acceptable fallback positions from the packet. Record who can settle each point.

## nda-triage

Identify scope of confidential information, exclusions, term, permitted recipients and return requirements. Flag one-way versus mutual coverage and deviations from the supplied baseline.

## rfp-builder

Translate requirements into comparable supplier questions with units, response format and evaluation weights. Separate exclusion criteria from scored preferences before responses arrive.

## supplier-scorecard

Normalize supplied supplier measures to comparable definitions. Apply documented weights after hard constraints and show which criterion drives the ranking. Flag missing evidence instead of treating it as zero.

## vendor-evaluation

Compare bids on total comparable cost, service commitments, delivery and risk. Test one plausible sensitivity and identify what evidence the recommended bid still needs.

## vendor-management

Track committed service level, actual performance, supporting evidence and remediation dates for each supplier. Check whether a repeated breach changes the renewal decision.
