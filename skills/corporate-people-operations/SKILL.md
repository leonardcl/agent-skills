# Corporate people operations

Identify the decision owner and the observable job requirement or process outcome. Use anonymized records. Separate directly observed behavior from inference and choose an intervention whose effect can be measured without revealing an individual.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include compensation-benchmarking, dei-strategy, employee-engagement-survey, employee-handbook-builder.

## Try it

A fictional intake desk had 10 cases, four waiting on the same missing field.

**Expected result.** The improvement proposal identifies the form field, counts affected cases and defines the same measure for the next sample.

**Limit.** Small samples and personal records restrict what conclusions can be drawn.
