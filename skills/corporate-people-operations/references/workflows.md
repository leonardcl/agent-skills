# Corporate people operations workflows

## compensation-benchmarking

Compare anonymized role bands with matching level, geography, currency and compensation basis. Show sample size and median; flag weak comparability before inferring a gap.

## dei-strategy

Use aggregate, authorized process measures to locate barriers in access or participation. Suppress small identifiable cells. Choose an observable process change and a follow-up measurement.

## employee-engagement-survey

Draft neutral, single-topic items and define anonymity thresholds before reading responses. Report response rate and distribution alongside theme counts. Keep identifiable free text out of the summary.

## employee-handbook-builder

Separate approved policy text from proposed guidance. Give each section an owner, effective date and exception route. Mark location-dependent terms for qualified review.

## incident-postmortem

Build a timeline of a benign service outage from supplied timestamps. Separate contributing conditions from guesses. Give each corrective action an owner and a check that would have detected the same failure earlier.

## interview-kit-builder

Derive questions from job tasks and define observable scoring anchors before the interview. Use the same core exercise for each fictional candidate and record evidence separately from ratings.

## job-description-writer

List the outcomes a role owns, decisions it can make and evidence needed at entry. Separate essential requirements from skills that can be learned and verify every requirement relates to a real task.

## kaizen

Measure a recurring step, change one constraint and repeat with a comparable input. Track both throughput and downstream defects before proposing adoption.

## meeting-insights-analyzer

From a selected redacted meeting summary, extract decisions, unresolved questions and actions. Link each action to its owner and due condition and mark statements with unclear ownership.

## onboarding-planner

Map a new hire’s first independent task to prerequisites, training and an owner. Check access through simulated steps and define the observable result that marks readiness.

## performance-review-assistant

Group anonymized observations by agreed job outcomes. Cite a behavior and consequence for each assessment. Distinguish missing evidence from underperformance and propose a checkable next goal.

## process-optimization

Map arrival, queue, service and exit for one work item. Compare waiting with active time to choose a bottleneck. Pilot one change and verify the queue was not shifted to the next step.

## project-status-report

Compare completed artifacts with the last agreed milestone. Lead with delivery state, follow with evidence, blocker owner and next observable checkpoint. Separate forecast dates from commitments.

## resource-planning

Convert demand and available capacity to the same time unit. Identify the first overloaded period and test a shift in scope, allocation or date. Preserve minimum coverage in the revised plan.

## resume-generator

Match supplied experience to target requirements. Preserve verified dates, responsibilities and quantities. Draft achievements without adding credentials or implying ownership absent from the source.

## sop-builder

Write the trigger, inputs, responsible role, critical steps and exit evidence. Include a recovery route for a missing input and have a second reader try one disposable case.
