# Event film

Prepare a short event film using approved footage, timing and on-screen copy.

## Workflow

1. Verify usage rights for each clip and check factual text against the supplied event brief.
2. Assign each shot a job: orient the viewer, show the activity or present the action. Place the date where it can be read over the real frames.
3. Watch the sequence muted and with sound. Check text against bright backgrounds and record any shot that needs a replacement.

## Try it

Make a short invitation from three approved clips of a public reading room.

**Expected result.** A shot-by-shot edit plan, timing and a final review tied to the actual frames.

For a six-second invitation, try two seconds of the room opening, two showing a reader selecting a book and two holding the supplied event date. Decode the export at each cut. If the door opens across the date halfway through its hold, shift the title to a stable shot and replay at normal speed. Keep an unconfirmed guest name out of the title even if the footage is cleared for use.

**Limit.** The user must supply approved footage and event details before a publishable export.
