# Indonesian edit

Edit Indonesian copy for register, natural phrasing and clear instructions.

## Workflow

1. Choose Anda or kamu from the audience and keep it consistent. Identify borrowed sentence structures and unnecessary nominal forms.
2. Use a direct verb where it preserves meaning. Check separate di for location and joined di- for the passive.
3. Read the final sentence alongside its context and verify numbers, names and dates against the source.

## Try it

Revise “Silakan melakukan pengecekan jadwal Anda” for a formal notice.

**Expected result.** For this notice, “Silakan periksa jadwal Anda” keeps the formal register and the requested action.

Compare “Jadwal tersedia di kantor” with “Jadwal diperbarui setiap Senin.” The first names a place. In the second, the prefix belongs to the verb. Preserve the source’s frequency and form of address when you simplify the surrounding sentence.

**Limit.** Specialist terminology and exact quotations can warrant retaining a less common form.
