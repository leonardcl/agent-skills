# Marketing acquisition

Match channel, target query or placement to audience intent. Ground claims in the supplied product facts. Draft an offline artifact and define a measurement boundary before proposing distribution or spend.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include ad-creative, ads, ai-seo, aso.

## Try it

A sample help site has two pages that answer the same query and one broken internal route to the signup form.

**Expected result.** Identify which page should carry the primary answer, how the other signals its role and how to verify the signup route locally.

**Limit.** The exercises cover analysis and drafts. They do not submit listings, purchase ads or publish campaigns.
