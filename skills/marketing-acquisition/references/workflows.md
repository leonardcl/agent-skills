# Marketing acquisition workflows

## ad-creative

Match one product benefit to a specific audience objection. Draft a headline, visual brief and destination promise using only supported facts. Compare two variants that differ on one meaningful creative choice. See the [detailed guidance](ad-creative-detail.md).

## ads

Build an offline campaign plan around the desired event, audience, budget ceiling and expected funnel. Calculate the break-even cost per acquisition and test whether sample campaign rows reconcile to spend. Leave activation outside the draft. See the [detailed guidance](ads-detail.md).

## ai-seo

Test whether a supplied page answers a precise question with a source, date and unambiguous entity. Compare quoted claims with the page text and distinguish visible citation evidence from an assumption about a search system.

## aso

Compare supplied app listing text and screenshots against a specific search intent. Map each claim to a real feature and propose a listing experiment whose outcome can be measured from comparable impressions.

## co-marketing

Draft a local joint brief showing each fictional partner’s audience, contribution, deliverable and approval owner. Identify the overlap that makes the collaboration worthwhile and a metric both can verify. See the [detailed guidance](co-marketing-detail.md).

## community-marketing

Identify the member problem and a recurring contribution that solves it. Plan a small community session and compare meaningful participation against raw membership. Draft materials locally. See the [detailed guidance](community-marketing-detail.md).

## competitive-ads-extractor

Annotate a supplied public advertisement by audience, promise, supporting evidence, format and call to action. Separate visible content from inferred targeting. Keep a citation and propose original wording.

## competitors

Draft an alternatives page around the buyer’s criteria. Compare like plans and dates, give a case where the alternative is a better fit, and verify every claim from current supplied evidence. See the [detailed guidance](competitors-detail.md).

## content-research-writer

Build an outline from the reader’s question and the supplied source packet. For each material claim record source and scope, then draft the passage with missing evidence marked.

## content-strategy

Map audience questions to intent and stage. Choose a gap a supplied source can substantiate, then specify the piece, distribution assumption and next measurable reader action. See the [detailed guidance](content-strategy-detail.md).

## directory-submissions

Prepare an offline listing inventory with fit, required fields, canonical URL and approval status. Deduplicate by audience and destination. Validate draft entries locally without submitting them.

## events

Choose the audience, decision and event format before planning promotion. Prepare a brief, run of show and post-event measurement plan. Distinguish registrants, attendees and qualified outcomes.

## free-tools

Pick a small recurring customer task and define a tool that gives a useful result without a sales interaction. Test one known input, a boundary case and the route from result to next action.

## image

Draft an original image brief with subject, composition, dimensions, usage rights and text-safe area. Work with locally selected cleared assets or simple geometry and inspect the actual crop.

## influencer-marketing

Score fictional creator profiles for audience fit, evidence quality and brand constraints. Distinguish engagement from reach and produce an internal campaign brief without contact or paid placement.

## launch

Define the audience, release readiness, assets, owners and rollback or delay condition. Trace the journey from announcement draft to a working destination and separate readiness from actual publication.

## lead-magnets

Choose one practical question a downloadable artifact resolves. Deliver a sample result, identify the information truly needed to receive it and record the intended path from download to activation.

## link-building

Find a relevant information gap inside supplied public source examples. Design an original citation-worthy resource and a local list of suitable contexts. Verify that every proposed citation actually supports the surrounding claim.

## programmatic-seo

Check whether each row in a local dataset supplies a genuinely different answer. Generate two sample pages and an incomplete-row case. Reject empty combinations and identify canonical or consolidation rules. See the [detailed guidance](programmatic-seo-detail.md).

## public-relations

Separate confirmed facts, attributable quotes and open questions. Draft a local announcement or holding statement appropriate to the audience and name the evidence required before release.

## schema

Select structured data matching the page’s visible content. Build a minimal local JSON-LD sample, parse it and compare names, dates and URLs with the rendered text. Flag claims about external search presentation as unverified.

## seo-audit

Inspect a supplied local page set for index intent, reachable links, canonical targets, descriptive titles and content matching the promised query. Prioritize a broken user route over cosmetic metadata changes.

## site-architecture

Group pages by user intent and choose relationships from the task, not the URL alone. Walk from an entry page to a target and back, then identify orphans, loops and duplicated destinations.

## social

Use an approved fact to draft for a named channel, format and audience. Preserve the claim when shortening, make the next action possible and define the denominator for any engagement comparison. See the [detailed guidance](social-detail.md).

## twitter-algorithm-optimizer

Compare supplied public posts using the same observation window, exposure denominator and audience. Separate content changes from distribution changes. Treat platform ranking claims as hypotheses.

## video

Choose the audience question, the visible proof and the delivery format. Plan a short sequence using cleared local footage, verify the call to action while muted and check text over the actual frames.
