# Marketing positioning

Select a customer segment, its problem, a credible alternative and the evidence for the differentiator. Determine which claim the supplied evidence supports. Record a counterexample and a low-cost way to test the proposed message.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include brand-guidelines, brand-voice-enforcement, competitor-profiling, copywriting.

## Try it

Ten fictional research notes include four buyers mentioning setup time and two mentioning cost. No purchasing study was run.

**Expected result.** The brief cites the counts and proposes a setup-oriented message test without claiming that most buyers prefer it.

**Limit.** Draft positioning and forecasts need separate market validation.
