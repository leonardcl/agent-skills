# Marketing positioning workflows

## brand-guidelines

Translate the supplied brand sample into practical rules for names, tone, color roles and exceptions. Apply each rule to a fresh sample and identify where the source leaves room for judgment.

## brand-voice-enforcement

Compare a draft with approved voice samples. Mark an exact phrase, the competing rule and a revision that preserves the original fact. Leave quotations and required terminology intact.

## competitor-profiling

Compare public product claims, target segments, proof and constraints from a supplied packet. Label observed positioning separately from inferred strategy and cite the evidence for each difference.

## copywriting

Order the page around the buyer’s decision, supporting fact, relevant objection and next available action. Replace every unsubstantiated benefit with supplied evidence or an explicitly unanswered question. See the [detailed guidance](copywriting-detail.md).

## discover-brand

Compare approved examples, audience descriptions and product evidence. Identify recurring voice or visual choices with a counterexample. Keep observed practice separate from proposed direction.

## domain-name-brainstormer

Generate pronounceable name candidates from the brief and test spelling, ambiguity and distinctiveness. Mark all legal and registration availability as unverified unless evidence is supplied.

## marketing-council

Evaluate one decision from the customer, economics and distribution perspectives. Summarize their separate objections and choose a reversible experiment that could change the recommendation.

## marketing-ideas

Generate options grounded in one audience constraint, an available asset and a measurable result. Rank by expected learning and effort, then give the smallest experiment that distinguishes the top choices.

## marketing-loops

Map the input, user action, resulting output and how it creates the next input. Quantify each transition on an example cohort and identify a break in the proposed feedback loop.

## marketing-plan

Define the business objective, audience, baseline and binding resource. Select channels that reach that audience, allocate an explicit budget and set milestones with decision thresholds.

## marketing-psychology

State a plausible behavioral mechanism and the observation supporting it. Draft a transparent design change that preserves choice and compare it with a simpler explanation on an offline fixture.

## offers

Specify the outcome, audience, deliverables, exclusions and credible evidence. Compare two offers at an equal total price and identify the uncertainty a pilot would resolve.

## pricing

Choose a value metric users can predict and the business can measure. Compare representative usage levels across tiers, show the effective price and identify a discontinuity or misunderstood limit. See the [detailed guidance](pricing-detail.md).

## product-marketing

Record the selected segment, motivating problem, alternatives, evidence and exclusions. Distinguish supported benefits from proposed positioning, then apply the same facts to a brief and a short message. See the [detailed guidance](product-marketing-detail.md).

## reverse-engineer-marketing

From supplied public pages, trace the visible promise, entry point, conversion path and follow-on content. Cite what can be observed and label presumed spend, targeting and conversion rates as unknown. See the [detailed guidance](reverse-engineer-marketing-detail.md).
