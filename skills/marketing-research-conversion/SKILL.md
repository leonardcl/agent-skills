# Marketing research conversion

Define a single conversion event, eligible population and observation window. Reconcile the funnel before blaming a page. Link interview evidence to a proposed mechanism, then write one change, expected direction and guardrail. Keep observations and forecasts separate.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include ab-testing, analytics, attribution, conversion-tracking.

See the [worked funnel calculation](references/funnel-walkthrough.md) for the arithmetic and decision boundary.

## Try it

A fictional signup page has 200 eligible visits, 40 form starts and 10 completions. Four interview notes mention uncertainty about a required field.

**Expected result.** Report 20 percent start rate, 25 percent completion among starters and 5 percent overall conversion. Treat a clearer field label as a hypothesis.

**Limit.** A funnel and interviews identify a candidate experiment. They do not demonstrate a causal lift.
