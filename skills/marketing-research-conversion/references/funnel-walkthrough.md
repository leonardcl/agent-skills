# A form-label experiment

A sample has 200 eligible visit sessions, 40 form-start sessions and 10 completed sessions. Start rate is 40/200, or 20 percent. Completion among starters is 10/40, or 25 percent. End-to-end conversion is 10/200, or 5 percent. Those are three different questions.

Four selected interviewees misunderstood one required field. Check that the form actually requires it and that their examples came from the same version. Draft an alternative label or example that keeps the input requirement unchanged. Assign eligible sessions consistently and choose one primary measure in advance. Track invalid submissions as a guardrail. A second report of one person's confusion is still one participant.

If starts increase while completions stay at 10, report the changed start rate and the unchanged number of completions. An apparent improvement at one step does not establish an end-to-end gain.
