# Motion edit review

Review a rendered sequence for caption readability, motivated motion and continuity.

## Workflow

1. Read the cut list and note the first and last frames of the caption. Watch once at normal speed.
2. Check the caption against the brightest underlying frames and the actual action. Describe the concrete timing or contrast failure.
3. Move the cut or caption boundary, render the affected passage again, and compare the same frames.

## Try it

A two-line caption appears just before a cut in a silent how-to video.

**Expected result.** A timecoded finding and a revised sequence where the whole sentence can be read over the intended shot.

Record the caption's last complete reading frame and the cut frame. When they coincide, move the caption or edit point and replay the whole sentence at normal speed.

**Limit.** Frame inspection alone cannot confirm how an audio cue feels at playback speed.
