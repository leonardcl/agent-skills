# Motion opportunities

Identify interface changes that would benefit from motion.

## Workflow

1. Trace the user's attention before and after each change. Identify objects whose relationship becomes hard to follow.
2. Propose motion only when it communicates continuity, cause or feedback. Give a reduced-motion equivalent.
3. Rank proposals by the confusion they resolve and include the evidence that would invalidate the recommendation.

## Try it

A list reorders after a filter and a static title is also suggested for animation.

**Expected result.** A ranked proposal for the reorder and a reason for keeping the static title still.

The reordered list has a continuity problem a small transition can explain. A stationary title needs an independent reason to move.

**Limit.** A proposal does not establish that the resulting animation performs well.
