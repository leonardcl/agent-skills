# Passage study

Build a structured study note for a selected biblical passage.

## Workflow

1. Identify the passage boundaries, translation and literary setting. Read the surrounding section before interpreting isolated words.
2. Separate observations from interpretation. Tie each discussion question to a verse or a documented translation difference.
3. Add a short application question and mark competing readings explicitly. Link the study into the chosen notebook if requested.

## Try it

Prepare a study of a short passage about hospitality using a supplied translation.

**Expected result.** A passage outline, cited observations and questions readers can answer from the text.

A verse-level observation can be compared directly with the supplied translation. A proposed cultural explanation belongs under interpretation with its source marked.

**Limit.** Use quotations only within the translation's permissions and identify interpretive traditions when relevant.
