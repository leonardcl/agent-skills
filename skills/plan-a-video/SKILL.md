# Plan a video

Plan and review a short video from source material through a rendered sequence.

## Workflow

1. List the essential visible steps, their approximate durations and the source shot for each.
2. Build a timed sequence and place captions where the viewer can see the action. Reserve enough reading time at actual playback speed.
3. Review frames immediately before and after each cut, then watch the silent export without the script.

## Try it

Show how to fold a paper bookmark in a silent twenty-second clip.

**Expected result.** A shot list, timing map and notes tied to actual frames where a fold or caption is unclear.

A folding shot earns its duration when the viewer can see where the edge meets the crease. Check the final pre-cut frame and the first frame of the next shot for that detail.

**Limit.** A storyboard cannot verify timing or legibility of an unrendered export.
