# Practice lesson

Build a short lesson around a skill the learner can demonstrate.

Use this workflow when the user selects it or specifically asks for this deliverable.

## Workflow

1. Ask for a prediction using a small list, then show how each statistic is calculated.
2. Change one outlier and ask the learner to explain the difference before revealing the result.
3. Use a fresh example as a transfer check and record the next practice item from the learner's actual response.

## Try it

Teach a beginner to distinguish a mean from a median.

**Expected result.** A worked example, a learner attempt and a new problem with a checkable answer.

For 1, 2, 3 the mean and median both equal 2. Replace 3 with 30 and ask for a prediction. The mean becomes 11 while the median remains 2.

**Limit.** Exposure to an explanation does not demonstrate mastery.
