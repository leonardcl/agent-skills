# Practice lesson

Build a short lesson around a skill the learner can demonstrate.

Use this workflow when the user selects it or specifically asks for this deliverable.

## Workflow

Choose a task the learner can carry out unaided and ask for a prediction before demonstrating it. If the learner already succeeds, change a relevant condition to find the edge of their understanding. If they struggle, work one example and have them explain the step that changed the outcome.

Use different inputs for the independent attempt. If a learner correctly predicts that the mean changes when an outlier grows but also moves the median, have them sort the values and point to the middle position. Then try a fresh odd-length list without the hint. Record what they did on their own separately from what they did with prompting.

## Try it

Teach a beginner to distinguish a mean from a median.

**Expected result.** A worked example, a learner attempt and a new problem with a checkable answer.

For 1, 2, 3 the mean and median both equal 2. Replace 3 with 30 and ask for a prediction. The mean becomes 11 while the median remains 2.

**Limit.** Exposure to an explanation does not demonstrate mastery.
