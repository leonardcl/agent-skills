# Public market research

Identify instrument, as-of date, period, currency, units and data source before making a comparison. Align like periods and label reported, consensus and modeled values separately. For missing live data, request a public export or work the stated offline case. Report calculation and sensitivity, never a transaction.

Choose the [task procedure](references/workflows.md) matching the requested outcome. Available topics include catalyst-calendar, company-valuation, earnings-preview, earnings-recap.

See the [worked quarterly comparison](references/earnings-walkthrough.md) for the arithmetic and decision boundary.

## Try it

A fictional firm reports revenue of 108 million against a same-period estimate of 100 million. The prior year was 90 million.

**Expected result.** The recap reports an 8 percent positive surprise and 20 percent year-on-year growth with different denominators.

**Limit.** No broker, account, paid feed or live order capability is bundled. Synthetic figures support method verification only.
