# Public market research workflows

## catalyst-calendar

List confirmed public event dates and tentative windows separately. Record the metric each event could change, the public source date and the next observation. Keep estimates provisional when timing is unknown.

## company-valuation

Choose a method consistent with what the firm earns and owns. Show enterprise-to-equity adjustments separately, compute per-share value only with a matching share count, and compare a downside assumption.

## earnings-preview

Freeze period, consensus timestamp, prior results and key drivers before the release. Build base and sensitivity cases. Identify which reported line would confirm or refute the driver hypothesis.

## earnings-recap

Compare reported results with the matching pre-release estimate and prior-year period. Compute surprise and growth with their respective bases. Separate management guidance from the analyst’s explanation.

## estimate-analysis

Compare two estimate vintages for the same fiscal period. Show the absolute revision, percentage change and contributor count. Flag changes in coverage that could explain apparent consensus movement.

## etf-premium

Align market price and net asset value for the same share class, currency and valuation time. Compute (price minus NAV) divided by NAV. Flag stale underlying markets before interpreting a premium.

## finance-sentiment

Code selected public excerpts by source, time and stated view. Separate repeated syndication from independent observations. Show the denominator and a contradictory example before summarizing sentiment.

## fintel-data

Use a supplied dated ownership or short-interest export. Distinguish reported positions, outstanding shares and float. Recompute a percentage using its documented denominator and flag publication lag.

## generative-ui

Choose a view from the research question, such as a dated comparison table or return series. Bind every displayed figure to a local row and preserve units, missing values and source dates when filtering.

## hyperliquid-reader

Normalize a supplied public market snapshot by instrument, quote currency and timestamp. Distinguish last trade, mark price, open interest and funding. Report available fields and provenance without wallet access or order capabilities.

## idea-generation

Generate research questions by comparing peers on one consistently defined metric. Separate screening signals from explanations. List a public filing or supplied time series that could disprove each explanation.

## initiating-coverage

Build a research baseline from the business model, segment mix, period financials, peers and uncertainty. Reconcile headline figures to the underlying reported statements and date each forecast.

## morning-note

Set an explicit as-of cutoff and comparison close. Separate verified releases, price moves and open questions. Attribute each claim to a timestamped public record and leave unavailable prices blank.

## options-payoff

Draw an illustrative expiration payoff from publicly supplied strike, premium and terminal-price scenarios. For a long call, subtract the premium from max(terminal price minus strike, zero), then apply any stated contract multiplier. Report assumptions without proposing a trade.

## public-excerpts

Use only selected public or fictional excerpts supplied as files. Keep authoring date, public source label, sample window and whether an excerpt quotes another source. Group duplicate syndication. Report claims and counterexamples separately. Private channels, account readers and live collection are outside this edition.

## saas-valuation-compression

Compare enterprise value to consistent trailing or forward revenue across two dates. Separate changes in the numerator and denominator. Compare margins and growth using the same period and label causal explanations as hypotheses.

## sector-overview

Define the sector boundary and choose comparable public metrics. Separate market-wide drivers from firm-specific changes. Date each source and identify where definitions differ across peers.

## sepa-strategy

Use a supplied historical price series to check stated trend-screen conditions at a fixed cutoff. Compute indicators from data available then, include failed candidates and handle insufficient history separately. Produce a retrospective research table.

## spglobal-funding-digest

Deduplicate supplied public funding announcements by company, round and event date. Separate announced totals from undisclosed rounds. Summarize by period without treating an unknown amount as zero.

## spglobal-tear-sheet

Create a dated one-page company snapshot from supplied public data. Show fiscal period, valuation date, comparable units and key risks. Reconcile price times shares to the stated market value or explain the gap.

## startup-analysis

Use the supplied public product, customer and financial evidence to distinguish traction, funding and projected demand. Reconcile cash runway assumptions and give one uncertainty that a subsequent filing or update could settle.

## stock-correlation

Align overlapping return dates and use comparable adjusted prices. Calculate correlation of returns rather than levels. Report sample size, window and differences when dates or outliers are excluded.

## stock-liquidity

Using supplied public observations, distinguish shares traded from currency value traded and spread from volatility. Summarize a consistent time window and label missing quotes before drawing comparisons.

## thesis-tracker

Store the original thesis, date, supporting metric, contrary evidence and next checkpoint. Append new observations rather than rewriting the initial prediction. Show which assumption changed.

## tradingview-reader

Read a supplied OHLCV export, identify timezone and bar interval and check high/low consistency. Show the last complete bar separately from an unfinished one. Live charts and account sessions are optional upstream services.

## yc-reader

Compare supplied public company-directory rows by cohort, description and last update. Treat a directory description as a company claim and look for independent support before interpreting traction.

## yfinance-data

Read a previously supplied public price table. Record ticker, adjustment convention, interval and as-of time. Check split or dividend adjustment consistency before calculating returns.
