# Shot analysis

Analyze a supplied video shot by shot using visible and audible evidence.

## Workflow

1. Mark cut times and label each base shot from what is visible. Separate camera movement, subject motion and graphics.
2. Record caption entry and exit, audio cues and uncertain production methods. Use frame samples around disputed cuts.
3. For each shot list the source asset, camera or edit operation needed for a comparable original sequence.

## Try it

Explain the cuts, camera motion and captions in a short craft demonstration.

**Expected result.** A timecoded shot table with evidence, uncertainty and a production plan for new footage.

A centered subject growing on screen supports a push-in or a crop. Describe both possibilities until parallax or source footage distinguishes them.

**Limit.** A rendered clip rarely establishes the exact software, model or source library.
