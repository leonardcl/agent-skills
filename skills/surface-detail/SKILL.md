# Surface detail

Review the details that distinguish interactive surfaces and their states.

## Workflow

1. Map the visible affordance against the actual hit area. Inspect focus, hover, pressed and disabled cues.
2. Choose one truthful interaction model and make text, spacing, icon and state feedback agree with it.
3. Drive the control by keyboard and pointer and check the revised response at the edges of the target.

## Try it

A card appears clickable over its whole surface but only its title is active.

**Expected result.** Visual cues, focusable region and actual hit area agree for the card.

Click the card edge and activate its focused element with Return. Those results establish whether its styling accurately describes the activation target.

**Limit.** A screenshot alone cannot establish event handling.
